# Privacy Policy for Tasbeeh Counter Pro

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Tasbeeh Counter Pro ("the app") is a dhikr counter with prayer times and a Qibla compass. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

The app does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only in local storage on your phone:

- your dhikr counts, custom dhikr, history and statistics
- your settings, daily goal and reminders
- the location you choose for prayer times (city or coordinates)

This data is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

## Location

If you tap "My location", the app asks for location permission and reads your approximate or precise position once. It uses it on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. To show a place name, the device's built-in geocoding service (provided by Google on Android, Apple on iOS) may be contacted with your coordinates, under that provider's own privacy policy. You can deny location permission and pick a city from the list instead; every feature still works.

The Qibla compass reads the device compass sensor while the Qibla screen is open. Sensor readings are not stored or sent anywhere.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders or prayer alerts, the app asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. No push server is involved.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/devasick/Tasbeeh or contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
